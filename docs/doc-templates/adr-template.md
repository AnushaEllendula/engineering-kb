# ADR-00XX: <Decision title, phrased as an action>

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-00YY
- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Deciders:** <names or team>

## Context

What is the issue we're facing? What forces (technical, organizational, time) are at
play? Write this so someone with no memory of the discussion can understand why a
decision was needed at all.

## Decision

State the decision clearly, in one or two sentences up top, then elaborate.

## Alternatives considered

List the realistic alternatives and why each was not chosen. This is often the most
useful part of an ADR six months later.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result of this decision? Include negative
consequences and trade-offs honestly — an ADR that only lists upsides isn't trustworthy.
